THE FAQ

Can I order food from this website? ＋

This is a frontend demo. The menu and cart are sample pages and do not process real orders or payments.

Are the offers real? ＋

The offers are examples to demonstrate the design. They are not connected to a live restaurant or payment system.

Why do some sections use video? ＋

Short looping videos add movement to selected sections. They may need an internet connection to load.

Can this website be hosted online? ＋

Yes. The static HTML and CSS pages can be hosted on GitHub Pages, Netlify or another static hosting service.
